# Pricing Hub App — Video Walkthrough

A 2+ minute screen-share demo of the **Urban Hamster Pricing Hub** for the
finance/sales team, showing how the app meets Kent's request. The video shows
both the presenter and the screen; no code is shown.

## Speaker Notes

**Intro (on camera).** Our reps negotiate with clothing vendors every week
without good product data in hand. This app fixes that — pricing, margins, and
sales in one place, filterable by department, category, and brand.

**Walkthrough (screen-share).**
- **Top of app** — the title states the purpose; the four KPIs show the full
  catalog with no filters: 29,120 products, ~$10.8M total sales, ~51% average
  profit margin.
- **Three charts** — retail price distribution (most products under $200, a tail
  toward premium items), profit-margin spread (clustered 40–60%, nothing below
  ~33%, consistent with the cost-plus pricing rule), and product count by
  category.
- **The negotiation use case** — apply a filter (a brand or category) and watch
  the KPIs, all three charts, and the data table update instantly to that
  vendor's products. Before a meeting, a rep can see how many products we carry,
  the margins we earn, and the revenue they drive — that's the leverage.
- **Data table** — every field (price, cost, margin in $ and %, total sales) for
  the filtered selection.

**Close.** Pricing, margins, and revenue in one filterable view — built to walk
into vendor negotiations with the data on our side.
